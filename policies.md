# Airline Policies

## Basic Economy
Non-refundable, non-changeable, no cancellations allowed. No free baggage.
Check-in is required 2 hours prior strictly.

## Standard
Changeable with a $50 fee, cancellable for airline credit. Cannot be refunded to original payment method. 
Includes 1 free carry-on. No checked bags.

## Refundable / Flexible
Fully refundable to original payment method up to 24 hours before flight. Changes are free. Includes 2 free checked bags.
Priority boarding included.

## Force Majeure Exceptions
If a flight is cancelled by the airline due to weather or technical issues, ALL passengers regardless of fare type receive a full refund to their original payment method or an equivalent voucher + 20% bonus.
